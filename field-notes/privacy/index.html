Privacy Policy for Field Notes Watch Face

Field Notes Watch Face is developed by Spare Hours.

Field Notes does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information or user data.

The watch face may display information provided locally by Wear OS and installed system data providers, including weather, calendar events, step count, heart rate, and battery status. This information is processed and displayed on the user's device and is not transmitted to Spare Hours.

Field Notes does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, user accounts, cloud storage, or a backend service.

Because Spare Hours does not collect or store user data, there is no user data retained by Spare Hours and therefore no user data to delete.

Field Notes may open system or provider applications such as Calendar, Alarm, health applications, or battery settings when the user taps supported areas of the watch face. Those applications are governed by their own privacy policies.

If the data handling of Field Notes changes in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
