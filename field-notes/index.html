About Field Notes

Field Notes is a Wear OS watch face inspired by the character of a well-used notebook. It combines a hand-drawn visual style with a clear time display and locally supplied watch information.

Multiple visual themes let the watch face fit different moods while keeping the same simple, handwritten character.
