Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to current Spare Hours products, including Field Notes Watch Face and Tiny World Watch Face, unless a future product-specific privacy notice states otherwise.

Information handling

No user account is required to use these watch faces. Spare Hours does not operate a backend service for these products, and no personal data is transmitted to Spare Hours.

Device and watch data used by a watch face, such as steps, battery level, calendar information, weather or heart rate where applicable, is processed locally for display on the user's device. Spare Hours does not collect this information.

These products do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. They do not collect location data.

Data retention

Because these products do not transmit user data to Spare Hours and Spare Hours does not operate a backend for them, there is no server-side user-data retention for these products.

Other services

A watch face may display information supplied by Wear OS, installed system data providers or other applications, and may open a provider application or system setting when the user taps a supported area. Those services and applications are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If the data handling of a Spare Hours product changes, this policy or a product-specific notice will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact Spare Hours at sparehours.dev@gmail.com.
